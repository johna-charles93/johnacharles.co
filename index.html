I build businesses and the systems that grow them. As CEO of Orbit One, I lead a full-service digital agency at the intersection of AI, marketing, and technology — helping brands scale through SEO, SEM, automation, and high-performance web development.

Beyond the agency, I'm actively building Ohio Gas Supply and Roush Service Company. My focus has always been on results, but the tools have evolved — and I've evolved with them. Over a decade in, I'm more focused than ever on what's next.
